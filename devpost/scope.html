Project scope · Proof of concept

Sahyog AI

A rapid community disaster and relief coordination app that uses Gemini AI to help prioritize local emergencies and coordinate relief.

Scope approved

The idea in one flow

One connected proof: a report becomes an AI-assisted recommendation that responders can review and track.

The unique kernel

Gemini turns a citizen's urgent local report into a severity score and step-by-step dispatch recommendation, helping local responders prioritize what needs attention.

Who it's for

Citizens and victims needing immediate help or essentials, plus local NGO teams, volunteers, and rescue coordinators organizing a response.

The core loop

Submit a location, emergency category, and description; review the urgency assessment and recommended next steps; track the report as active or resolved.

Identity & motivation

Fast, clear, and practical. The learner wants to build Sahyog AI and get a working proof of concept. Chosen stack: Python, Streamlit, and Google Gemini.

What "working" looks like

In a short demo, submit an emergency and see Gemini return urgency and actionable guidance, then find that report in the operations view with its status.

Safety boundary

This proof of concept demonstrates coordination; it does not replace official emergency services or provide authoritative dispatch instructions.

The boundary

Now · prove the core idea
  • Emergency form for location, medical/food/water/shelter category, and crisis description.
  • Gemini urgency scoring when configured; otherwise a clearly labeled local rule estimate, not presented as AI.
  • Operations dashboard for active/resolved reports and volunteer status.
  • A connected report-to-assessment-to-operations demo flow.
  • Optional sample-data preview without an API key; examples are not Gemini output.
Later · beyond this proof
  • Real-time coordination and operational deployment for actual emergencies.
  • More complete nearby volunteer and supply matching.
Cut · not what this demo claims
  • Replacing official emergency services or presenting AI output as authoritative dispatch instructions.
