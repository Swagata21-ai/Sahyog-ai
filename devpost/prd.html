Product requirements · Sahyog AI

From report to response

A rapid community disaster and relief coordination app for citizens needing help and local responders coordinating relief.

PRD approved

The core journey

1

Open

Enter an emergency report or review the operations dashboard.

2

Describe

Provide location, choose a category, and describe the crisis.

3

Assess

Gemini returns an urgency score and step-by-step recommendation.

4

Coordinate

Responders review the report and AI output in operations.

5

Resolve

Update status to resolved, or return it to active if needed.

Surfaces & experience

Emergency report

One form collects location, Medical/Food/Water/Shelter category, and a crisis description. Gemini is used when configured; otherwise a transparent local rule estimate is labeled non-AI. Missing required details get a clear prompt.

Operations dashboard

Reports show category, location, description, assessment source, urgency, recommendation, and status. Responders can focus on active or resolved reports and change a report's status. Sample cases can be loaded without a key and are never shown as Gemini output.

Look and feel

Fast, clear, and practical for an emergency context. Make status and urgency easy to distinguish. No specific font or visual reference was provided.

Volunteer status

Show a simple volunteer-status view. Do not imply volunteers are verified, available, or assigned; full matching is outside this proof of concept.

Important safety boundary

AI output and local rule estimates are informational only—not validated triage or authoritative medical, rescue, or dispatch instruction. This demo is not an emergency service. Anyone facing immediate danger should contact local emergency services.

Behavior & proof

Submit a report and receive AI assessment

On valid submission, use Gemini when configured or transparent local rules without a key. Show the estimate and coordination steps with their source; never present local rules as AI.

  • A valid report receives a Gemini-generated score and recommendation.
  • Without a key, a report receives a clearly labeled local-rules estimate and rationale.
  • The user can identify the urgency and recommendation after submission.
  • Missing input is explained and does not create a report.
  • Gemini failure is visible, offers retry, and is not shown as a successful assessment.
  • After a Gemini failure, local rules are used only if the user explicitly chooses that alternative.
Review and update relief operations

A successful report appears in operations as active. Responders can mark it resolved or return it to active.

  • Submitted details, AI assessment, recommendation, and active status appear together.
  • Status changes are reflected in the dashboard and corresponding status view.
  • An empty dashboard explains that reports appear after submission.
  • Volunteer status is visible without implying assignment or availability.
  • Sample reports can be loaded and cleared without a key; sample assessments are clearly labeled as non-AI examples.
Failure, empty, and demo states
  • Show a clear empty state before the first report.
  • Indicate when Gemini analysis is in progress.
  • On unusable or failed AI output, explain the failure and provide retry.
  • Without a key, report submission uses local rules, labeled non-AI and not validated triage; users can also load a separate sample dataset.
  • The proof demonstrates reports in the current app run; no durable storage or live multi-user synchronization is promised.

Included in this proof

  • Emergency report form and validation
  • Gemini urgency assessment and recommendation
  • Operations dashboard with active/resolved status
  • No-key sample preview, clearly distinct from Gemini output
  • Volunteer-status display and clear safety messaging

Deferred or not a goal

  • Durable storage and live synchronization
  • Verified volunteer assignment or supply matching
  • Real-world dispatch or emergency-service replacement
